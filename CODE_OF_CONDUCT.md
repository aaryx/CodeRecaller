# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and patient. Welcome contributors of every background
and experience level. Critique code and ideas rather than people. Respect privacy,
boundaries and differing perspectives.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing and
deliberate disruption are unacceptable in issues, reviews, discussions and other
project spaces. Do not post credentials or another person's private data.

Contact project maintainers privately through the hosting platform to report a
problem. Maintainers will review reports confidentially where practical, seek
context and apply proportionate moderation: clarification, warning, content
removal, temporary restriction or permanent exclusion. Maintainers are also
accountable to these expectations; conflicts of interest should be disclosed.
